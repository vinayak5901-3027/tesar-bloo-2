FAQ

Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know about the launch and what comes after.

When will the products launch?
+
Bloo Crucible and Bloo SynthAI are targeted for General Availability in 2026. Pre-registered users will be notified first with a dedicated launch-day email and calendar invite.
Will there be a free demo after launch?
+
Yes. Every pre-registered user is eligible for a FREE personalised product demo with the Bloo product team. Check the box on the registration form and we'll reach out immediately after launch to schedule yours.
Can I request enterprise pricing?
+
Absolutely. Pre-registered users receive exclusive early-access pricing locked in before public announcement. Contact our team through the registration form and select your company size for a tailored quote.
Will product walkthrough recordings be available?
+
Yes. Full product walkthrough recordings will be published on our YouTube channel on launch day. Pre-registered subscribers will receive direct links as part of the launch email.
How do I access the release notes?
+
Full release notes for both Crucible and SynthAI are available now on the Bloo platform website. Click the Release Notes buttons on this page to explore every feature, integration, and capability shipping in v1.0.
Is the platform self-hosted or cloud-based?
+
Both products are self-hosted on Kubernetes on AWS or Azure. Customer data — including investigation records, query payloads, and AI call content — never leaves your environment. Air-gapped deployments are supported via Ollama.
